Note for Group 6(Sample space and events & Venn Diagramms and The Algebra of events)
* Experiment : Any action or process whose outcome is subject to uncertainty/randomness
* Sample Space : Set that contains all the outcomes of the possibiility(i.e a dice has a SS of{1,2,3,4,5,6})
* Algebra of events : 
	- Complement of E : The set of all outcomes in S that are not contained in E
	- Union of A and B : denoted by a weird U, is the event of consisting of all outcomes that are either in A or B or in both Events
	- Intersection of A and B : Denoted by a weird upside down U, consists of both events that occurs in both A and B
	- Weird crossed O : Null event
	- Intersect A and B = O -> Mutually exclusive or disjointed events 
	- Union A and B = O -> Exhaustive events 
* Null events : "Empty" set, consisting of impossible outcomes
* Exhaustive : All outcome(Like sample space)
* Disjoint : unrelated bs
